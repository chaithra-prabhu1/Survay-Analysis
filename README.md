# Student Food Delivery Survey - Power BI Dashboard

Note: the data is simulated for practice, not a real survey.

## Goal
Understand which food delivery app students prefer, what problems they face and how satisfied they are.

## What I did
- Used 53 simulated practice survey responses and cleaned them in Excel (removed 2 test responses and 1 without consent) down to 50 valid ones
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard: KPI cards, pie chart of preferred app, bar chart of biggest problem, satisfaction charts, and slicers for frequency and bill amount

## Conclusion
- Swiggy is the most used app (52%) but has the LOWEST average satisfaction (3.38 vs Zomato 3.67)
- High fees are the biggest problem (44% of students)
- Average satisfaction is 3.5 out of 5 - users are only moderately happy, so apps have clear room to improve on fees and delivery
